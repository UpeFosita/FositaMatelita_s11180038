Case Study: Major Depressive Disorder (MDD)

Introduction
Major Depressive Disorder (MDD), or depression, is a mental disorder that affects mood, thoughts, and daily functioning.

Symptoms
Persistent sadness or emptiness
Loss of interest in activities once found pleasurable
Fatigue and low energy
Changes in appetite and sleep
Difficulty concentrating
Thoughts of suicide or self-injury

Possible Causes
MDD may be brought about by genetic factors, chemical imbalances in the brain (e.g., low serotonin levels), traumatic experiences, and chronic stress.

Treatment Options
Psychotherapy (e.g., Cognitive Behavioral Therapy - CBT)
Medication (e.g., Antidepressants like SSRIs and SNRIs)
Lifestyle Changes (exercise, healthy diet, sleep improvement)
Support Groups and Social Support
